# 8. AgroMarket: Farm-Fresh, Direct to You

**Concept:** A marketplace where local farmers sell produce directly to buyers.

## Basic Functionality

- List a product with price, stock, and harvest date.
- Buyers browse and purchase.
- Buyers leave a star rating.
- Sellers see their earnings.

## Tweaks

- Sort listings by freshness or price.
- Sellers get a low-stock alert.

## Additional Features

- Seller storefront pages showing all their products and overall rating.
- Order history and reorder option for repeat buyers.
- Category and seasonal browsing (vegetables, fruits, grains, dairy).
- Buyer wishlist/favorites for produce they want to track.
- Admin view of platform-wide sales and top-performing sellers.
- Delivery vs. pickup option selectable at checkout.

## Wow Factor: Produce Ages in Real Time

- Prices automatically drop as produce gets older, just like it would in real life.
- Past a certain age, the item is marked expired and can no longer be bought. No manual updating needed.
